Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM
  To create a fully constrained 2D sketch of the given mechanical component 
using Autodesk Fusion 360 by applying the specified dimensions, geometric 
constraints, and dimensional relationships accurately.

## REQUIREMENTS

 Hardware: 
* Processor: Intel Core i3 or above 
* RAM: Minimum 4 GB 
* Hard Disk: Minimum 10 GB free space 
* Monitor: 14-inch or larger display 
* Input Devices: Standard keyboard and 3-button mouse 
Software: 
* Operating System: Windows 10/11 (64-bit) or macOS 
* Software: Autodesk Fusion 360 – 
Educational/Commercial License
  
## PROCEDURE
Step 1: Create New Design and Set Units 
  ● Open Document Settings: Expand the Document Settings folder 
on the left-side Browser tree 
  ● Change Units: Hover over Units, click the Change Active Units 
icon (gear symbol), select Millimeter (mm) in the dialog box, and 
click OK 
  ● Activate Sketch: Click the Create Sketch button on the top-left 
toolbar 
  ● Select XY Plane: Pick the flat XY Plane from the 3D origin axes 
(represented by the red X and green Y axes on the grid floor) 
  ● Sketch Environment: Fusion automatically rotates your camera to 
a flat, straight-on 2D view showing the center origin point (0,0,0), 
the grid, the Sketch Palette on the right, and the 2D sketch toolbar.

Step 2: Draw Reference/Construction Lines
     Choose the Line Tool (L) and turn on Construction mode. Draw one vertical and one horizontal construction line through the origin (0,0). These reference lines are used to maintain symmetry and accurately locate the different features.
<img width="436" height="472" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 122838" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b215d902-887b-4a0b-81cb-e7e352b75ebf" />
Step 3: Construct the Main Hub and Internal Pattern
          Turn off Construction mode and create two concentric circles at the origin having Ø40 mm and Ø66 mm. Draw an R6 mm circle on the inner circle and create a circular pattern of 6 instances about the origin. Trim the unwanted portions to obtain the required internal spline/cutout arrangement.

  Step 4: Add the Main and Side Bosses
    Locate the upper boss at 65 mm above the origin and draw concentric circles of Ø20 mm and Ø32 mm.
    Locate the lower boss 50 mm below the origin and draw Ø10 mm and Ø20 mm circles. 
    Position the side bosses at 47 mm horizontally and 26 mm below the origin, with inner Ø14 mm and outer Ø20 mm circles.
<img width="647" height="622" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 124554" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/827ae4e8-8041-4bda-bbd1-11adb8d483d1" />
Step 5: Form the Outer Profile Using Arcs
Create connecting arcs between the central hub and the upper boss  using R64 mm arcs and apply tangent constraints.
Similarly, connect the central hub with the left and right bosses using R20 mm tangent arcs.
Add the required connecting arcs between the side bosses and bottom boss with the specified radii.

Step 6: Clean and Complete the Sketch
         Use the Trim Tool (T) to remove unnecessary portions of circles and arcs. Check the dimensions, alignment, symmetry, and tangent constraints. Ensure that the required profile is closed and fully constrained, then select Finish Sketch.
Step 7: Extrude the Completed Profile
        Select Create → Extrude (E) and choose the required closed profile. Exclude the internal holes and cutouts from the selected region. Enter the required thickness, such as 10 mm, select New Body, and click OK to obtain the final 3D component.
